# Interesting Prompts

This file collects prompts that demonstrate a specific or unusual application
response, such as rendered Markdown, retrieved image evidence, abstention, or a
notable confidence result. A prompt prefixed by a button label and colon is
available through that example-question shortcut in the UI. A prompt without a
prefix must be entered manually.

## Test or show Markdown rendering in retrieved chunks

Flight evidence: "What evidence is required for a flight reimbursement?"

Unsupported transfer: "Can an employee claim a helicopter transfer from airport to hotel?"

## Show `airport_transfer_eligibility_decision_tree.png`

Prague taxi after 21:00: "Can I take a taxi from Prague airport after 21:00?"

"Are the travel policies cool?"
